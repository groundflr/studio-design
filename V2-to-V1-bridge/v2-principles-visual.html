The atom: one modal card, everywhere

seed · used by both surfaces

The single highest-leverage thing to standardise. A grade edit, a flag, a role change, an invite, an audit-entry detail — all open the same shell. Header states what it is; body varies by context; footer puts the destructive action left and the primary right. Build it once and V2 inherits it verbatim.

  • H

  • B
    Body varies — the only part that changes between a grade, a flag, a user, an invite. Excerpt + score + adjustment here; fields elsewhere.
  • F
    Footer is fixed — destructive-left, primary-right. Justification gates the primary in adjust contexts.
